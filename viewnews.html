Makueni Hosts Annual Cultural Festival

Makueni County recently held its most vibrant cultural celebration, attracting thousands of visitors across Kenya and beyond. The event, hosted in Wote, showcased traditional dances, music, and crafts from the Kamba community.

Governor Mutula Kilonzo Jr. praised the turnout and emphasized the importance of preserving local heritage while promoting tourism. The event also featured local food markets and youth cultural competitions.

Organizers confirmed plans to make the festival an annual event on the county calendar to boost eco-tourism and cultural exchange.
